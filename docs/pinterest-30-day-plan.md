## 🚀 Quick Execution Guide for the Webmaster / Social Media Manager

1. **How to Generate & Download the 1000×1500 Pinterest Image:**
   - Click the **Direct Study URL** listed in any pin specification below.
   - On the webpage, click the **"📌 Save Study Card (Pinterest PNG)"** button in the hero area.
   - The interactive canvas modal will render a high-resolution 1000×1500 px vertical study card with colored pinyin tone contours, vocabulary collocations, character stroke previews, and branding.
   - Click **"💾 Download PNG (1000×1500)"** to save the image file locally to your device.
   - Click **"📋 Copy Pin Title & Description"** to copy the pre-formatted text directly to your clipboard.

2. **Pinterest Boards Structure to Create:**
   - `HSK 1 Vocabulary & Flashcards`
   - `HSK 2 & 3 Chinese Vocabulary`
   - `Mandarin Chinese Grammar Rules`
   - `Learn Chinese Study Tips & Cheat Sheets`

3. **Recommended Daily Pinning Rhythm:**
   - **Morning Slot (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM local target time):** Post **Pin A (Vocabulary Word Card)**.
   - **Evening Slot (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM local target time):** Post **Pin B (Grammar Rule Formula or Academic Masterclass)**.
